The Last Acorn · Family Guide

How do I get the words off Tom?

Look for the box called Turkey anatomy. On a computer, it’s to the right of the picture. On a phone, scroll just below the picture.

The box and the words on Tom disappear together. To bring them back, press Show anatomy above the picture.

Open the game

Just want to watch?

Turn up your volume and press ▶ Play story beneath the picture. Tom will gobble, and the story will play by itself. At the end, press Watch again for an encore.

Want to play with Tom?

Press Try a move, then choose Fan tail, Fly to log, or Rake leaves. For another loud gobble, press Gobble!

How do I win the Golden Acorn?

  1. Press Anatomy hunt. Read the clue above the woodland picture.
  2. Choose the move named in the clue. Then tap the body part that answers it. If Tom moves too fast, use the answer buttons in the Turkey anatomy box.
  3. Find all three parts to earn your badge. Press Download badge (PNG) to save it as a picture.
